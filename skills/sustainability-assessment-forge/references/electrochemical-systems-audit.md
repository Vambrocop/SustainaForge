# Electrochemical Systems Audit

Use this reference for electrolysis, electrosynthesis, electrochemical upgrading, and similar papers.

Performance metrics to capture:

- current density;
- cell voltage;
- Faradaic efficiency or selectivity;
- single-pass conversion;
- product concentration;
- stability duration;
- electrode area and geometry;
- electrolyte composition;
- membrane type;
- temperature and pressure.

Questions that often decide sustainability relevance:

- Is the cell voltage realistic at the reported current density?
- Are ohmic losses, crossover, and separations visible?
- Is product purification modeled?
- Is chloride, salt, solvent, or electrolyte management included?
- Are membrane and catalyst lifetimes reported or assumed?
- Is the electricity source explicit?
- Does the analysis distinguish stack performance from full process performance?

Watch for these moves:

- treating high current density alone as scale-up readiness;
- using short stability runs as if they imply durable operation;
- quoting low product cost without balance-of-plant and separation costs;
- ignoring gas compression, drying, recycle, or downstream purification;
- assuming ideal renewable power without discussing intermittency or capacity factor.

Interpretation rule:

An electrochemical paper can be scientifically strong while still being incomplete as a sustainability assessment. Keep those judgments separate.
