# Uncertainty, Scenario, and Scale-Up

Use this reference when the result depends on unstable assumptions.

Separate three layers:

1. Parameter uncertainty
2. Scenario uncertainty
3. Scale-up uncertainty

Parameter uncertainty includes:

- yield;
- conversion;
- selectivity;
- electricity price;
- feedstock price;
- uptime;
- catalyst or membrane lifetime;
- capital scaling factor.

Scenario uncertainty includes:

- grid electricity versus renewable electricity;
- present-day versus target future performance;
- different coproduct treatment rules;
- regional logistics and transport cases;
- conservative versus optimistic commercialization environments.

Scale-up uncertainty includes:

- whether the observed lab result persists at area, volume, throughput, and time scales that matter;
- whether supporting infrastructure creates penalties absent from the lab setup;
- whether operating windows narrow under real process control and impurity loads.

Good practice:

- identify dominant variables;
- show at least a baseline, conservative, and optimistic scenario;
- explain whether uncertainty changes ranking or only magnitude;
- say when conclusions are conditional on future technology improvement.

Bad practice:

- reporting only one best-case point estimate;
- presenting future-target cases as if they were current performance;
- treating scale-up as a single multiplicative factor with no process consequences.
