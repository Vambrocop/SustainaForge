# Scale-Up Readiness Checklist

Mark each item as `yes`, `partial`, `no`, or `unclear`.

## Process Basis

- functional unit remains meaningful at larger scale:
- reference flow can be reconstructed:
- comparator is industrially relevant:

## Performance Persistence

- productivity shown beyond a short proof-of-concept run:
- stability duration is decision-relevant:
- key losses are quantified:

## System Completeness

- separations are visible:
- balance-of-plant is visible:
- recycle and purge streams are discussed:
- utilities are included:

## Materials and Equipment

- catalyst lifetime is discussed:
- membrane or separator lifetime is discussed:
- corrosion or compatibility risks are discussed:
- critical materials dependency is discussed:

## Commercial Translation

- plant scale is explicitly modeled:
- utilization assumptions are explicit:
- cost basis is reproducible:
- uncertainty changes are shown:
- scenario dependence is acknowledged:

## Bottom-Line Note

Write one sentence on the main reason the scale-up claim is or is not persuasive.
